# 12 Principles of Animation

The **12 Basic Principles of Animation** are the foundational toolkit of
motion — a set of guidelines developed by Disney's Nine Old Men (Ollie
Johnston and Frank Thomas) and collected in *The Illusion of Life: Disney
Animation* (1981), widely regarded as the "Bible of animation."

## What's inside

A complete, self-contained reference to all 12 principles — definition,
purpose, examples, and practical notes — organized so you can study one,
apply it, and move on. Each principle includes a visual reference and a
suggested example.

## The 12 Principles

| # | Principle | English | PT / ES |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Squash and Stretch | Esticar e Comprimir | Aplastamiento y Estiramiento |
| 2 | Anticipation | Antecipação | Anticipación |
| 3 | Staging | Encenação | Escenografía |
| 4 | Straight Ahead Action and Pose to Pose | Siga direto e Pose a pose | Straight Ahead e Pose to Pose |
| 5 | Follow Through and Overlapping Action | Prosseguimento e Ação de sobreposição | Follow Through e Overlapping Action |
| 6 | Slow In and Slow Out | Começar e terminar devagar | Slow In y Slow Out |
| 7 | Arcs | Arcos | Arcos |
| 8 | Secondary Action | Ação secundária | Secondary Action |
| 9 | Timing | Cronometragem | Timing |
| 10 | Exaggeration | Exagero | Exageración |
| 11 | Solid Drawing | Desenhar sólidos | Solid Drawing |
| 12 | Appeal | Apelo | Atração / Campanha |

## How to use this

1. **Pick a principle** from the list above.
2. **Read** its definition and purpose.
3. **Try** it in your own animation (even a 10-frame loop).
4. **Log** what you tested and what you learned in
   `templates/study-log.md`.

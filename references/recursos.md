## Study suggestions

1. Repeat a ball fall and observe squash and stretch;
2. Animate a jump without anticipation, then with — compare;
3. Show a primary action with and without secondary action;
4. Animate a character "twins" mirrored to see the effect;
5. Test the "appeal" of two different compositions of the same pose, with
   the same character.

## How to contribute

This skill is derived from public-domain / CC-BY-SA sources. You can
contribute more:
- test environments (Blender, After Effects, Spine, etc.);
- real-world exercises (walk loop, pick-up, roll, etc.);
- graphical ports for each principle.

# Animation principles study log

Model notebook for recording exercises, what worked, and what did not work,
while practicing the 12 principles.

## How to use

1. Choose a principle and date the exercise.
2. Note the objective, source of reference, and what you observed.
3. Record the group: before/during/after.
4. At the end, answer: "What did I learn?" and "What will I improve?"

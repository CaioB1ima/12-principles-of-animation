# Kinetic type — a word in motion

A short study of a single word animated with the 12 principles.

## The word: "motion"

### Breakdown

| Step | Action | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Letters drift thin (1–2 frames) | Anticipation |
| 2 | Letters converge along an arc | Arcs |
| 3 | The "o" stamps with a squash on impact | Squash & Stretch |
| 4 | The letters hold, then release | Slow In & Slow Out |
| 5 | A color wash follows | Follow Through / Secondary Action |

### Guidance
- Keep the word legible; legibility is part of staging and appeal.
- Time each letter to the audio beat.
- Limit the exclamation — one clear, memorable moment.

# Motion Graphics — example

Kinetic typography and a logo reveal, built from the 12 principles.

## 1. Kinetic typography

### The word "motion"
1. **Anticipation:** the letters drift thin (1–2 frames).
2. **Arcs:** the letters converge along an arc into the word.
3. **Timing:** each letter lands on the beat of the audio.
4. **Squash & Stretch:** the "o" stamps with a slight squash on impact.
5. **Appeal:** the word is legible, clear, and on-brand.

## 2. Logo reveal
1. **Staging:** the brand mark is the only thing moving; the background is
   neutral.
2. **Follow Through:** a spark or line follows the logo after it settles.
3. **Secondary Action:** a color wash or particle field animates after the
   logo, supporting it.

## 3. Workflow

1. Block the key poses (start, peak, final).
2. Add the intermediate motion and timing.
3. Add the easing and arcs.
4. Refine with secondary action.

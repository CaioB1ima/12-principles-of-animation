# Character Animation — example

A walk cycle and a jump, built from the 12 principles. Use the timing charts
to rehearse before animating.

## 1. Walk cycle (12 frames per cycle)

| Frame | Pose | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contact L | Anticipation (lean, breath) |
| 2 | Contact R | Overarching follow-through |
| 3 | Passing L | Arcs (leg swings) |
| 4 | Passing R | Overlapping (arms vs. legs) |
| 5 | Down | Slow-out, slight squash |
| 6 | Down | Weight shift |
| 7 | Up | Weight shift continues |
| 8 | Up | Squat lift prepares |
| 9 | Contact R | Anticipation (lean) |
| 10 | Contact L | Overarching follow-through |
| 11 | Passing L | Arcs |
| 12 | Passing R | Overlapping |

### Forces
- **Gravity:** the body falls and recovers.
- **Weight:** the heavier the walk, the fewer frames per step.
- **Squash & Stretch:** slight squash on the load phase; stretch on the
  recovery phase.

## 2. Jump (timing chart)

| Frame | Pose | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crouch | Anticipation |
| 2 | Crouch | Squash (loading) |
| 3 | Takeoff | Speed up (slow-out) |
| 4 | Peak | Apex (slow in) |
| 5 | Peak | Peak hold |
| 6 | Descent | Slow-out |
| 7 | Landing | Squash + follow-through |
| 8 | Settle | Hold |

### Forces
- **Timing:** 8–12 frames for a light jump; 14–20 frames for a heavy one.
- **Arcs:** the trajectory follows an arc, not a straight line.
- **Squash & Stretch:** the landing compresses; the body elongates on the
  ascent.
